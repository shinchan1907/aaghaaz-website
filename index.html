Every woman deserves a beginning.

Aaghaaz – A Beginning works for the betterment of less-privileged women — professional training in stitching, English-speaking and beautification that leads to independence, dignity, and the power to support their families.

Our mission

A single goal: women who
shape their own change.

Here at Aaghaaz, we are driven by a single goal — to empower women to initiate personal and social change, and to do our part in making the world a better place for all.

The journey

Three steps. One beginning.

This is how change happens at Aaghaaz — a path our women walk, from their first lesson to earning with dignity.

01

Learn

Professional training in stitching, English-speaking and beautification — practical skills that open the door to employment.

02

Earn

Aaghaaz Boutique employs our alumni — turning extraordinary designing and stitching skills into livelihoods.

03

Empower

With skills and income come independence and dignity — the power to support their families and shape their own change.

What we do

Programs that turn skills
into livelihoods.

Vocational Training

Professional training in stitching, English-speaking and beautification — practical skills that open doors to employment and financial independence.

Aaghaaz Boutique

Our alumni put their extraordinary designing and stitching skills to work, creating beautiful clothes for women — employment built on everything they learned.

Aaghaaz Magazine

Our annual magazine celebrates the journey — asking bold questions, challenging deep-rooted gender biases, and honouring everyone who supports the cause.
