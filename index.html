Our Story

Driven by a single goal — to empower women to initiate personal and social change.

Aaghaaz – A Beginning is a non-profit organisation based in Jalandhar, Punjab. We work for the betterment of less-privileged women by providing them different forms of training and helping them to become independent and support their families.

From the classroom to the boutique to the printed page, everything we do is built around one belief: when a woman earns, a family rises — and a community changes.

Founded by Nidhi Kohli

What we do

Programs that open doors

Three paths, one destination — independence.

Vocational Training

Training in stitching, English-speaking and beautification — teaching girls employable skills that lead to employment and financial independence.

Aaghaaz Boutique

Our alumni are employed using their designing and stitching skills — crafting beautiful clothes for women, and turning creativity into livelihood.

Training in focus

Three skills. Countless new beginnings.

  • StitchingFrom first seam to finished garment — a trade she owns for life.
  • English-speakingThe confidence to speak, apply, interview — and be heard.
  • BeautificationProfessional beauty skills that open doors to salons and self-employment.

Aaghaaz Boutique

Designed & stitched by our alumni.

Women trained at Aaghaaz are employed in our boutique, using their designing and stitching skills to create beautiful clothes for women — every garment carries a story of independence.

Aaghaaz Magazine

In print, their voices.

Once a year, we publish the Aaghaaz Magazine — a celebration of what our women have achieved, and an honest questioning of the gender biases they continue to face.

It is part yearbook, part mirror: proof of how far training and determination can carry a woman, and a reminder of how far society still has to go.
