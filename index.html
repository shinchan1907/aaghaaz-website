Our story

One goal: women who shape their own change.

Here at Aaghaaz, we are driven by a single goal — to empower women to initiate personal and social change. We want to do our part in making the world a better place for all.

We strive to build productive relationships and make a positive impact with all of our pursuits — from vocational classrooms to our boutique and our annual magazine.

Aaghaaz Magazine

Celebrating every beginning.

Aaghaaz was founded with the hope of steering society towards gender equality. With our annual magazine, we honour everyone who supports Aaghaaz and celebrate how far we have come — one story at a time.

From stage performances by our girls to the launch of Anokhi Dukaan, the magazine captures the moments that make the mission real.
